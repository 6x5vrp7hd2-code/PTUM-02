HSEQ and operational excellence

Health, safety and quality

Electrical safety is part of how work is planned, controlled, carried out and handed over.

Our electrical safety principles

Discipline before energisation

Plan

Understand the system

Review the scope of work, network condition, interfaces and hazards before any activity begins.

Control

Define responsibility

Establish clear operational ownership, configuration and safe working boundaries.

Isolate

Identify every source

Consider alternative supplies, potential backfeeds and changing network conditions.

Earth

Establish safe conditions

Apply and control the appropriate earthing throughout the work.

Authorise

Control access

Ensure work proceeds under the correct electrical safety documentation.

Monitor

Maintain oversight

Monitor permit holders and changing project conditions throughout delivery.

People

Competent individuals with clearly defined responsibilities.

Process

Consistent operational procedures and disciplined documentation.

Systems

Accurate understanding and control of the electrical network configuration.

Communication

Clear interfaces between operational, construction, commissioning and client teams.
